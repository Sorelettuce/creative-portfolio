What the check measures

Start with the file’s origin

The 3-inch export spans −49.4 mm to +26.8 mm horizontally. A 90 mm field extends from −45 mm to +45 mm, so the recorded placement crosses its left edge by 4.4 mm. Centering moves the bounds 11.3 mm to the right and leaves 6.9 mm to each edge.

Compare a size change

The larger export measures 96.0 × 96.2 mm. Even centered, it extends 3.1 mm past the 90 mm field on the vertical axis. The 100 mm field leaves 1.9 mm at its tightest edge.

This comparison uses the nominal rectangular stitch field. On the machine, confirm the working area and any required clearance for the actual setup.
