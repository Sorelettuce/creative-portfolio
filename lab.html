Evaluation notes

Reproduce the failure

Set the expected behavior

The sphere starts at −3.60 m and moves at +56 m/s toward a 0.20 m wall centered at 0 m. Its radius is 0.26 m. First contact should occur when the center reaches −0.36 m.

Observe the endpoint check

A sampled overlap test sees the sphere clear of the wall at both ends of the step. Its path crossed the wall between samples, so it finishes at +3.40 m without an overlap event. This is tunneling.

Use a swept test as the reference

Check the movement segment against the wall’s near face, offset by the sphere’s radius. The intersection gives first contact at 57.9 ms. Apply a restitution coefficient of 0.65 and use the remaining time to advance the rebound. The sphere ends at −2.80 m.

Prototype scope

Review the result

The two runs share geometry and simulated timing, so the solver is the variable under test. The reference is limited to a single sphere moving on one axis toward a static wall. The outcome appears in text as well as in the scene, and reduced-motion mode shows the result immediately.
