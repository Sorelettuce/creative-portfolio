Process

Make the next action visible

Show when inventory satisfies an order

As I played, it was easy to collect a required item and miss that the order was ready. I asked for better in-context feedback for ready-to-use items and completed requirements so players could see progress from the commission itself.

Keep the merge loop moving

I also directed a change to remove the energy cap. That gave players room to keep experimenting with item combinations and reduced pauses between decisions on the board.

Iterate in the running game

I supplied the interaction direction and evaluated the changes in play. Codex implemented the browser prototype and the resulting updates.
